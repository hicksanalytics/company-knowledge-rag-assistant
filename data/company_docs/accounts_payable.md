# Accounts Payable Procedures

## Invoice Intake

Invoices are submitted to the Accounts Payable inbox. The team records the vendor name, invoice number, invoice date, purchase order, subtotal, tax, and total. Potential duplicates are held for review based on vendor, normalized invoice number, and amount.

## Approval Thresholds

Invoices up to $5,000 require approval from the department manager. Invoices above $5,000 and up to $25,000 also require director approval. Invoices above $25,000 require vice president approval. An approver may not approve an invoice they created for their own reimbursement.

## Payment Schedule

Approved invoices are included in the Tuesday and Thursday payment runs. Accounts Payable prioritizes valid early-payment discounts when cash planning permits. Exceptions require approval from the Controller.

## Record Retention

Invoices, approval evidence, and payment records are retained for seven years in the approved financial system.
