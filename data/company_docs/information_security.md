# Information Security Policy

## Data Classification

Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer financial information and authentication credentials are Restricted. Employees should store Confidential and Restricted information only in approved systems.

## Access Reviews

System owners review privileged access quarterly and ordinary user access at least annually. Access must follow least privilege and should be removed promptly when a worker changes roles or leaves the company.

## Security Incidents

Suspected phishing, lost devices, exposed credentials, or accidental disclosures must be reported immediately to the Security team through the incident hotline or security portal. Employees should not investigate or delete evidence on their own.

## Artificial Intelligence Tools

Employees may use only company-approved AI tools for company work. Restricted or Confidential data must not be entered into public AI services. AI-generated output must be reviewed by a responsible employee before it is used in a business decision or external communication.
