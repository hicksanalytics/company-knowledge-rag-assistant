# Vendor Management Standard

## New Vendors

The requesting department completes a vendor intake form and identifies the business owner. Procurement reviews pricing and contract terms. Information Security performs a risk review when a vendor will access confidential data or company systems. Finance creates the vendor record only after the business owner, Procurement, and all required risk reviewers approve the request.

## Changes to Payment Details

Accounts Payable must independently verify every request to change a vendor's bank or payment details using a known contact method already on file. Staff must not rely only on the email or phone number included in the change request.

## Annual Review

Business owners review critical vendors annually for service quality, continued need, security issues, and contract renewal dates.
