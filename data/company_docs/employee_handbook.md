# Cedarline Services Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off each calendar year. New employees accrue PTO beginning on their first day and may use accrued time after 30 days. Requests for one or two days should be submitted to the employee's manager at least five business days in advance. Requests for three or more consecutive days should be submitted at least ten business days in advance. Up to five unused PTO days may carry into the next calendar year; additional unused days expire on December 31.

## Holidays

The company observes New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. The People Operations calendar is the authoritative source when a holiday falls on a weekend.

## Remote Work

Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Employees must use company-managed devices, protect confidential information from household members and visitors, and connect through the approved secure access method.

## Sick Leave

Employees should notify their manager before the start of the workday when illness prevents them from working. Sick time is recorded against available PTO unless state or local law requires a separate leave category.
