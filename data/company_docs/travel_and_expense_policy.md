# Travel and Expense Policy

## Expense Reports

Employees must submit expense reports within ten calendar days after a trip ends. Every expense of $25 or more requires an itemized receipt. The employee's direct manager approves ordinary expenses; Finance also reviews any report above $2,000.

## Airfare and Lodging

Travelers should book economy airfare and reasonably priced lodging through the approved travel portal. Premium cabins require written approval from an executive before booking. Hotel internet and parking are reimbursable when needed for business.

## Meals and Mileage

Business meals are reimbursed up to $75 per person per day unless a client event is approved in advance. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS business mileage rate. Commuting between home and a regular work location is not reimbursable.
